Questions

Is this a real marketplace?

Not yet. Vouchly Beta tests whether people want this. Payments are simulated and your listings are saved only in your browser.

How do I know a code is valid?

The full version will verify codes and hold payouts during a dispute window. Never rely on a code shown in this beta.

Is reselling vouchers allowed?

Many offers say they are non-transferable. Sellers must check the issuer's terms before listing.

What can't I list?

Stolen, expired, redeemed or duplicate codes, account logins, personal data, and anything illegal. See the Prohibited Items policy.

Do you share account passwords?

No. Most services forbid sharing logins, and it can get accounts banned. Group plans use the service's official seat or invite feature, so each member signs in with their own account.

Which items can I list in the beta?

Gift cards, single-use coupons and transferable subscription vouchers up to ₹5,000 each, plus seats in official group plans. Other categories are closed for now.

What are the fees and taxes?

Sellers pay a 5% fee on completed sales. Sellers are responsible for their own income tax and GST. See the Seller Agreement.

How do I report a problem?

Use the report link on a listing, or contact the Grievance Officer. Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours.
